# About the model — plain-language explainers

These are the human-readable companions to the technical record in
[`../MODEL.md`](../MODEL.md). They explain, without the maths, what the tool does,
what it deliberately doesn't claim, and the surprising amount of testing that sits
behind a deliberately simple algorithm.

| Artifact | What it covers |
|----------|----------------|
| [what-it-is.md](what-it-is.md) | What the model is, what it is **not**, and how to use it sensibly. |
| [what-we-tested.md](what-we-tested.md) | Everything we tried to make it smarter — and why the simple version won. |
| [live-results.md](live-results.md) | The week-by-week scorecard: are the "value" picks actually any good? *(fills in over the season)* |

**The one-sentence version:** it's a tidy, honest research input for someone who's
going to have a bet anyway — a sensible projection and a conservative "floor" for
each player — and it is **not** a system for beating the bookies.

These are living documents. The third one updates as the automated weekly scorecard
accumulates real results; once a month or so of rounds is in, the three together
are meant to stand alone as the full, honest explanation of the model.
