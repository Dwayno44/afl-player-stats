# Live results: is the floor holding?

This is the honesty page. Every round the tool records its own disposal **floors**
*before* the games are played, then grades them *after* against what actually
happened — automatically, with no cherry-picking. Over a season it builds into a
real track record.

**The thing that matters: does the floor hold?** The floor is a conservative minimum
each player should clear about **85%** of the time. If the "shown" floor-hit rate
sits near 85% round after round, the tool is doing its core job. *(So far, it is.)*

The **green/amber tints** are a secondary, honest extra — where our model disagrees
with the bookie's price. They're a research *input*, not an edge (see
[what-we-tested.md](what-we-tested.md)). The one useful nuance: the **green** ("clear
value") picks have cleared their floor *less* often than amber, so if you use the
tints, favour amber.

## How to read the table

- **Floor-hit** — share of "shown" picks (floor of 10+) where the player cleared
  their floor. Target ≈ 85%. This is the number that matters.
- **Value picks** — how many tinted picks that round, so you can see the sample grow.

## What we expect this to show

If the next month looks like the early signs:

- the **floor keeps landing near 85%** — the tool is honestly calibrated, and the
  "minimum" you see is a fair one; and
- the **green tints keep underperforming amber** — confirming that the model's
  biggest "edges" are the least reliable picks, so amber (or un-highlighted) is the
  safer lean.

The tints are a research input, not a betting system (see
[what-we-tested.md](what-we-tested.md)). The whole point of doing this in the open is
that the numbers, not our opinions, get the final say.
