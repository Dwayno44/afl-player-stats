A note on the value highlights

Separate from the floor: the green/amber tints flag where our model thinks the bookie’s price is generous. Be clear-eyed about them — they’re a research input, not a system for beating the bookies. A useful nuance: a bigger model edge usually means the market knows late info we don’t, so the green picks have actually cleared their floor less often than amber. If you use the tints, favour amber (or amber + un‑highlighted).
